Icons for the quick pill and its dropdowns. Drawn for basic-hypr on a 16 px
grid in a Papirus / Material style. White on transparent: bar/SvgIcon.qml
tints them to a Theme colour at runtime, so they follow the colour themes.

Exception: wired.svg is "computer-ethernet" from Hugeicons' free set
(MIT License, https://hugeicons.com), fetched via Iconify and recoloured white.

Also from Google's Material Symbols (Apache License 2.0), via Iconify, white:
bt-scan.svg (bluetooth-searching),
mode-saver.svg (eco), mode-perf.svg (speed).
